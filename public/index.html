Clickbait Blocker

A Chrome extension that hides clickbait links while you browse.

What the extension does

  • Adds a small mark button next to links on the pages you visit.
  • Keeps a shared list of links that users marked as clickbait, with up and down votes.
  • Hides links that reach a relevance score, and lets you reveal them again at any time.
  • Optionally summarises an article and estimates how likely its title is clickbait.

Signing in

The extension offers "Sign in with Google" so votes can be attributed to an account. Google sign in is used only to obtain your email address. No other Google account data is requested, and nothing from your Google account is posted or shared.

AI preview

Signed in users see an AI button next to the mark button. It sends the article URL to this server, which extracts the text of that article and returns a two sentence summary plus an estimate of how likely the title is clickbait.

The AI feature produces text only. Clickbait Blocker does not generate images, video or audio, and it does not create, request or distribute AI generated intimate imagery of any kind. Its only AI use is summarising pages the user is already reading.

Your data

The extension stores your email address and your votes so the shared list can credit them. See the privacy policy for details and for how to have your data removed.
