Privacy Policy

Clickbait Blocker Chrome extension. Last updated 25 November 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy describes the data handled by the Clickbait Blocker Chrome extension and by the server it talks to at clickbaitblockerapi.gotskillz.tech.

Data we store

  • Email address. Provided by Google sign in, used to identify your account and attribute your votes.
  • Votes. The link you marked or unmarked, the page it was found on, the page domain, and up or down vote counts.
  • Login timestamps. When the account was created and when it last signed in.

Your browsing history is not collected. The extension only reads pages you have open in order to place its buttons and to match links against the shared list.

Settings kept in your browser

Whitelisted domains, relevance thresholds, the list of links you personally marked, and your email are kept in the extension's local storage in your own browser. Removing the extension removes them.

Google account data

Clickbait Blocker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request the userinfo.email scope only, do not request any other Google data, and do not transfer Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms or data brokers.

AI preview

When you press the AI button, the article URL is sent to our server. The server fetches that page, extracts its text and sends a truncated version of the text to OpenAI to produce a summary and a clickbait estimate. Only the article text and title are sent. Your email address and identity are not included in that request. OpenAI's API terms apply to the processing performed on their side.

The AI feature produces text only. It does not generate images, video or audio, and it is not used to create or distribute intimate imagery of any kind.

Who can see the data

Votes are stored in a shared database and other users see aggregated vote counts for links. Your email address is not shown to other users. Data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database and is accessible only to the service operator.

Retention and removal

Account records and votes are kept while the service runs. To have your account and votes deleted, email us from the address you signed in with and we will remove them.

Cookies and tracking

The extension sets no cookies, runs no analytics and contains no advertising or tracking scripts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page changes as well.
